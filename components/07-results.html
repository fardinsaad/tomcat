Aggregates do not move
Across all six model–variant configurations, no P1–P4 mean departs from its own re-run by more than 0.075 on any metric, and none of the 35 defined omnibus tests reaches significance. Performance does not depend on surface form.

Exemplars and stability
🪨
Less drift, for GPT-4o
GPT-4o with Fs-CoT changes only 5–10 of 20 scenarios per condition against CP's 12–14 — the narrowest movement of any configuration. The effect is smaller for DeepSeek-R1 and does not hold for Gemma-3-27B.

Reading this together. P1–P3 were designed so that a memorising model would break and a reasoning model would not; none of them moves the aggregate. P4 changes the optimal plan by construction, and performance shifts in response, scenario by scenario. The pair rules out both failure modes at once: Tomcat is not reciting surface patterns, and it is not ignoring the grid.
