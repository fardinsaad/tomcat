The headline
Averaged over the four perturbations, every metric lands within 0.015 of the re-run baseline — and Action Optimality lands on it exactly. No comparison reaches significance. Aggregate performance does not depend on surface form.
But the items move
12–14 of 20 scenarios change on at least one metric in every condition. The stability is a cancellation, not an absence of effect — which is why the per-condition diagnosis below matters.
P3 caught it red-handed
In 10.txt and 10b.txt the agent collected the key at (5,5) — the exact cell of an unreachable distractor. Action Feasibility 0.0 in both. It matched on colour without checking the region.
P4 confirms the grid is read
Action Optimality rises +0.075. Split by the difficulty audit: scenarios marked easier gain +0.400, those marked harder lose 0.038 (p = .064). Performance tracks the geometry, so the maze is a real constraint.

Reading this together. P1–P3 were designed so that a memorising model would break and a reasoning model would not; none of them moves the aggregate. P4 was designed to move it, and it does — in the direction the difficulty audit predicts. The pair rules out both failure modes at once: Tomcat is not reciting surface patterns, and it is not ignoring the grid.
