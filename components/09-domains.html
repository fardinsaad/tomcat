🧭  Forced Coordination
The layout that makes the point. The two agents are sealed into complementary halves — the agent holds both onion dispensers and the only dish dispenser, the principal holds both pots and the only serving station. Neither can finish a soup alone, so every order has to be routed through a counter that touches both regions, and the principal’s position is the only cue for which counter they mean.

Scoped by the Associate Editor as “1–2 alternative environments plus simple perturbation checks”. Both halves are now complete. Overcooked reuses the prompt skeleton unchanged, so any change in performance is attributable to the domain rather than to a rewritten prompt.
