The Task
Instruction Inference
An agent assists a principal in reaching a goal by interpreting a single, often underspecified callout. Instruction types are grounded in Grice’s maxims: incomplete violates Quantity, ambiguous violates Manner.

Twenty scenarios over sixteen grid layouts — eight clear, six incomplete, and six ambiguous. The agent sees the grid, the principal’s movement, and the callout, then must produce an instruction type, a natural-language response, and an ordered action list. Three representative scenarios below; use the tabs to step through each one.
