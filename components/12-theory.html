Theoretical Account
Post-Training Paradigms, Not Product Names
An account naming three specific models cannot anticipate a fourth. We therefore restate it as a claim about what a model was optimised to do with an exemplar — a property of the training signal — and then test the prediction on models the account was not built on.Hover a paradigm for the models behind it.

The model is scored on reaching a correct final state and is free to choose its own route there. An exemplar then constrains a trajectory it has already optimised.

Six of seven agree. The seventh is the informative one. GPT-4o is the model the alignment account predicted would gain most, and it did in Doors, Keys and Gems — but its CP baseline in Overcooked is far stronger, so there is little headroom left for exemplars to recover. The account predicts how a model responds to an exemplar, not how much room remains for that response to show.
Paradigms are increasingly mixed: current systems combine preference alignment with an explicit reasoning phase, and their small positive movements are consistent with a model that both accepts a demonstrated format and has its own trajectory to protect. We therefore state the account as a prediction about direction, not magnitude.
