Second Environment
Overcooked
The same prompting method, the same six metrics, a different world. Twenty scenarios across seven kitchen layouts, seven models, two prompt variants — 280 annotated samples. Instruction inference now has to survive a domain where the principal moves, carries, and hands things over.

Seven kitchens

Five are the standard Overcooked-AI layouts; two were added for mechanisms the others do not contain — strict complementarity, and a single-tile choke point. Coordinates are the agent's only view of the world.

Two prompt variants

The skeleton is the one carried over from Doors, Keys and Gems, block for block. Only the contents change, so any difference in performance is attributable to the domain rather than to a rewritten prompt.
